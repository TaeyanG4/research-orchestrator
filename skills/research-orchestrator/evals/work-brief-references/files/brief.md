# Workshop preparation

Prepare an internal workshop in three dependent stages of work. This session
only establishes the project; nobody is to be contacted and no materials built.

First inventory the supplied teaching materials when they become available,
identify their owners and versions, and check which exercises have solutions.
Acceptance requires an inventory, version list, ownership gaps, and a list of
exercises missing solutions. Preserve all original material.

Next assemble a rehearsal packet from the approved inventory, check links and
exercise dependencies, and perform a local timed walkthrough. Acceptance
requires a packet, link report, dependency list, timing notes, and an issue log.

Finally prepare facilitator handoff after rehearsal issues are resolved.
Acceptance requires a run sheet, troubleshooting notes, unresolved questions,
and a readiness decision backed by the rehearsal evidence.

All work uses normal local resources. There are no assigned calendar dates.
